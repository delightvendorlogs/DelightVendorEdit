Need Answers?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using the marketplace.

How do I create an account?

Select Create Account, enter the requested details, and submit the form. Signup is intended to work without email confirmation when the Supabase Email provider is configured accordingly.

Create your account →

How can I buy a service?

Create an account or sign in first. Then choose an available service or approved seller product and follow the purchase instructions.

Get started →

How can I become a seller?

Create an account first, then complete the seller application. Your application and listing must be reviewed and approved before products are published.

Start here →

What is the seller commission?

The planned platform commission is 10% of an eligible seller sale. The seller commission policy explains how commission, order status, and payouts work.

How do I contact support?

You can contact support through WhatsApp, Telegram, or email. Use the Support section on this page to choose your preferred channel.

View support options →
Where can I read the policies?

The footer links to the terms, privacy, seller, referral, withdrawal and payout, and refund policies.
